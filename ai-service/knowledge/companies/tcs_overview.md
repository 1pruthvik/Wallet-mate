# Company Research: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is an Indian multinational information technology services and consulting company headquartered in Mumbai. It is a subsidiary of the Tata Group and operates in 150 locations across 50 countries.

## Business Strengths
- **Market Leadership**: Premier Global IT services provider with strong recurring enterprise revenue contracts.
- **High Return on Equity (ROE)**: Consistently generates robust ROE (>40%) and maintains a low debt-to-equity profile (<0.10).
- **Dividend Reliability**: High dividend payout ratio and strong free cash flow generation.

## Key Risk Factors
- **Global IT Spending Slowdown**: Macroeconomic headwinds in North America and Western Europe can impact discretionary IT budgets.
- **Currency Volatility**: Fluctuations in USD/INR and EUR/INR exchange rates impact net operating margins.
- **Attrition and Talent Costs**: Wage inflation in technical domains impacts operating margins.
