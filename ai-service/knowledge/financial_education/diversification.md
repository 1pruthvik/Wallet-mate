# Understanding Portfolio Diversification

Diversification is a risk management strategy that mixes a wide variety of investments within a portfolio. A diversified portfolio contains a mix of distinct asset types and investment vehicles in an attempt to limit exposure to any single asset or risk.

## Benefits of Diversification
- **Risk Reduction**: Spreading capital across different sectors and asset classes minimizes market volatility.
- **Capital Preservation**: Prevents severe drawdowns caused by poor performance in a single stock or sector.
- **Consistent Growth**: Balances high-growth opportunities with stable cash-flow assets.

## Core Asset Classes
1. **Equities**: Stocks offering growth potential and capital appreciation.
2. **Fixed Income**: Bonds and fixed deposits providing capital preservation and interest income.
3. **Cash & Cash Equivalents**: Short-term liquidity reserves for emergency funds and opportunistic buying.
