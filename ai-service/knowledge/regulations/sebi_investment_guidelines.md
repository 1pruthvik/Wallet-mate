# Regulatory Oversight: SEBI Guidelines on Investment Research

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulates securities markets in India to protect investor interests and promote market integrity.

## Key Regulatory Directives
- **Research Analysts Regulations**: Entails strict disclosures regarding conflicts of interest, personal holdings, and research methodology.
- **Risk Disclosures**: All investment research tools and algorithmic models must explicitly state risk factors, probabilistic nature, and lack of guaranteed returns.
- **Investor Protection**: Mandates clear segregation between independent educational tools and financial advice.
